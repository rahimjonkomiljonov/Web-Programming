How to Make Quinoa and Veggie Stir-Fry

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp grated ginger

Steps

  1. Heat sesame oil and sauté vegetables until tender.
  2. Add cooked quinoa and stir-fry briefly.
  3. Stir in soy sauce and ginger.
  4. Cook for 2 more minutes while stirring.
  5. Serve hot.
