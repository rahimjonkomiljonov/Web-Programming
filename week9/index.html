Accessibility Audit

I ran Lighthouse (Accessibility) in Chrome DevTools on my site and fixed the pages that didn't score 100.

What I found

12 pages scored below 100 on my Week 2 and Week 3 exercises. The screenshots below are from my own Chrome, on the live site, and the URL is in the address bar of each one. Both the before and after screenshots use the same URLs.

I took screenshots for 4 pages. The other 8 pages (week2/ex4, week2/ex7, week3/ex1, ex2, ex4, ex5, ex6 and ex7) had the same missing title problem, and the Week 2 ones also had no lang. They score 100 now too.

What I changed (code)

These are the exact changes, with the file each one was made in. Each fix removes a failing Lighthouse audit.

01

Missing page title

document-title

Where: 11 pages: week2/ex2, ex3, ex4, ex7 and week3/ex1 to ex7

Problem: The pages had no <title>, so the browser tab and screen readers had nothing to announce as the name of the page.

Fix: Added a descriptive <title> inside <head>.

02

Page was only a fragment: no html, head or lang

html-has-lang

Where: 4 pages: week2/ex2, ex3, ex4, ex7

Problem: These Week 2 files were bare fragments with no <html> tag, so there was no lang attribute (screen readers could not pick a pronunciation) and no <head> to hold a title.

Fix: Turned each one into a full document with <html lang="en">, a <head> with charset, viewport and title, and a <body>.

03

Low text contrast on the colour boxes

color-contrast

Where: week3/ex3.html

Problem: White labels on the red, amber, green and blue boxes measured between 2.14:1 and 3.76:1 in Lighthouse. Text needs at least 4.5:1.

Fix: Changed the label colour from white to black, which passes on all five boxes.

04

Low text contrast in the footer

color-contrast

Where: week3/ex8.html

Problem: The footer text from site-nav.js was #587078 on the page's dark #1e293b footer, which is 2.78:1.

Fix: Added a rule on this page that makes the footer text light (#cbd5e1) so it is readable on the dark background.

05

Touch targets too small

target-size

Where: week2/ex3.html

Problem: The menu links were only 17px tall. Lighthouse needs touch targets of at least 24px by 24px.

Fix: Made the links inline-block with a minimum height of 24px and a little padding.

06

No main landmark

landmark-one-main

Where: the 22 Week 2 and Week 3 exercise pages that were edited

Problem: DevTools flagged "Document does not have a main landmark". Screen reader users could not jump straight to the content.

Fix: Wrapped the page content in a <main> element.

07

Skip link

WCAG 2.4.1 Bypass Blocks

Where: every page that has a <main> (added once, in site-nav.js)

Problem: Keyboard users had to Tab through every link before reaching the content, and there was no way to skip them.

Fix: site-nav.js now adds a "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page. It stays off-screen until it gets keyboard focus.
