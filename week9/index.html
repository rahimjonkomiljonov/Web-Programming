Fixes

  1. Added a <main> landmark (landmark-one-main) to 22 pages, so screen reader users can jump straight to the content.
  2. Added lang="en" and a <title> (html-has-lang, document-title). Four Week 2 pages were bare fragments with no <html> or <head>, and seven Week 3 pages had no title. They now have a full document with a descriptive title.
  3. Fixed colour contrast (color-contrast). White text on the Week 3 colour swatches was 2.1:1 to 4.2:1, so the labels are now black (4.9:1 or better on all five). The footer on week3/ex8.html was 2.8:1 (#587078 on navy) and is now #cbd5e1.
  4. Enlarged touch targets (target-size). The menu links on week2/ex3.html were 17px tall and are now at least 24px.
  5. Added a skip link (WCAG 2.4.1, Level A). It sits at the top of the home page and the group project page. For every other page, site-nav.js adds one automatically. It stays off-screen until the first Tab, then jumps to #main-content.
