Accessibility Audit

I ran Lighthouse (Accessibility) in Chrome DevTools on my site and fixed the pages that didn't score 100.

What I found

12 pages scored below 100 on my Week 2 and Week 3 exercises. The screenshots below are from my own Chrome, on the live site, and the URL is in the address bar of each one. Both the before and after screenshots use the same URLs.

I took screenshots for 4 pages. The other 8 pages (week2/ex4, week2/ex7, week3/ex1, ex2, ex4, ex5, ex6 and ex7) had the same missing title problem, and the Week 2 ones also had no lang. They score 100 now too.

What I fixed

  • Missing page title: added a <title> to 11 pages. Four Week 2 pages had no <head> at all, so I turned them into full pages.
  • Missing language: added lang="en" to those four Week 2 pages.
  • No main landmark: wrapped the page content in a <main> tag.
  • Low contrast: the white text on the colour boxes in week3/ex3 was too light, so I made it black. The footer in week3/ex8 was too dark, so I made it lighter.
  • Small links: the menu links in week2/ex3 were too small to tap, so I made them bigger.
  • Skip link: I added a "Skip to main content" link to every page. It shows up when you press Tab.
