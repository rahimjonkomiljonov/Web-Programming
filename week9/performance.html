Performance Audit

I ran Lighthouse (Performance, Mobile) in Chrome DevTools on two of my slowest pages, made six changes, and ran it again. This is the second Week 9 homework; the accessibility one is here.

What I changed, in two sentences

I now host the fonts myself and preload them, load the two stylesheets at the same time instead of one after the other, minify the CSS, and show the big Week 9 screenshots as small lazy-loaded WebP images. That took the Week 9 page from 79 to 100 and the My Favourites page (week2/ex8) from 85 to 97.

What I changed (code)

01

Self-hosted fonts, preloaded

render-blocking-resources

Where: every page (the Google Fonts links) and assets/frame.css

Problem: Every page waited for fonts.googleapis.com before it could paint: two extra connections plus a stylesheet that is itself waiting for the font files.

Fix: I downloaded the three font families (latin subset, woff2, 115 KB in total), serve them from my own site, and preload the two that the top of every page uses. font-display: optional means the text never jumps when a font arrives late.

02

Stylesheets load side by side, not in a chain

render-blocking-resources

Where: assets/site.css and every page that uses it

Problem: site.css started with @import url("frame.css"). The browser had to download site.css first, read it, and only then ask for frame.css, so two round trips happened one after the other.

Fix: Removed the @import and linked both files in the page head, so the browser fetches them at the same time.

03

Minified CSS

unminified-css

Where: assets/site.css and assets/frame.css

Problem: The stylesheets were full of spaces, line breaks and comments that the browser does not need. Together they were 18,993 bytes.

Fix: The readable files now live in assets/src/, and a small script (tools/build-css.mjs) minifies them into the files the pages link to. The pair is now 14,004 bytes, 26% smaller. Here is the same rule before and after:

04

WebP images with fixed sizes, loaded lazily

modern-image-formats · offscreen-images · unsized-images

Where: week9/index.html (8 screenshots)

Problem: The Week 9 page loaded eight full-size PNG screenshots, 1,105 KB in total, even though they are shown about 460 px wide. That is why its Largest Contentful Paint was 5.4 s.

Fix: Each screenshot now has a 960 px WebP version (169 KB in total, an 85% saving). The page shows the WebP with width and height set, so nothing jumps, and loading="lazy" so pictures below the screen load later. A click still opens the full PNG.

05

Header that does not jump

cumulative-layout-shift

Where: assets/frame.css

Problem: On a phone, the header could grow from one line to two once the fonts arrived: the breadcrumb ("Week 2 / My Favourites") became wider and wrapped. Lighthouse blamed the whole page content, which ended up at 99 px instead of 63 px. That is the 0.222 layout shift on the My Favourites page.

Fix: On narrow screens the breadcrumb now always gets its own row, so the header is the same height before and after the fonts load.

06

Load the first picture first, the rest later

prioritize-lcp-image · offscreen-images

Where: week2/ex8.html

Problem: All three pictures started loading at the same time with the same priority, so the one you see first (the largest content on screen) had to share the connection with two pictures far below it.

Fix: The first picture gets fetchpriority="high". The other two are below the fold, so they get loading="lazy".

What I could not change

Lighthouse still says "use efficient cache lifetimes". GitHub Pages decides the Cache-Control headers, and I cannot set them there, so that note stays.
